## Who it's for

- New team leads finding their feet
- Engineering managers moving from doing the work to leading the people
  who do it
- Directors building and leading other managers
- First-time CTOs and VPs of Engineering stepping into the top seat

## What we typically work on

- **Stepping into a bigger role.** What to let go of, what to take on, and
  how to earn trust quickly.
- **Building a leadership team.** Hiring, growing and trusting the managers
  under you.
- **Delivery under pressure.** Getting a team to ship predictably without
  burning it out.
- **Difficult conversations.** Feedback, underperformance, conflict and
  saying no to stakeholders.
- **From engineer to manager.** Finding your new source of impact and
  satisfaction.

You bring the real situations you face that week. I bring structure, good
questions and, when it helps, my own experience from Seznam.cz, Kiwi.com,
Moravio, ThreatMark and IP Fabric.

## Format

- Packages of 6–10 sessions, 60 minutes each
- Usually every two to three weeks
- Remote over video, or in person in Brno and Prague
- In English, Czech or Slovak
- Paid by you or by your company
