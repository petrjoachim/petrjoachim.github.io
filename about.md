I've worked in tech since 2007, and I've led people almost from the start.
A few weeks into my first job as a support developer, I was the most
experienced person on the team and was looking after a few part-time
developers.

In 2008 I moved from Ostrava to Brno and joined Seznam.cz, then a fast-growing
company. Two years later I was running some of its services and my first
serious team.

The biggest turn came around 2018, when I went through two year-long
coaching programmes. They changed how I lead and how I work with people. At
Kiwi.com I moved from engineering into full-time leadership coaching and
coached engineering leaders while the company grew from 60 to 450 developers.
Leading Scrum teams under the LeSS framework shaped me just as much.

After that I went back to running engineering myself: as CTO at Moravio, VP
of Engineering at ThreatMark and Director of Product Engineering at IP Fabric,
where I've led up to 50 engineers. So when I coach CTOs and engineering
managers, I've usually been in a very similar seat.

## Background

- **Coaching:** two year-long coaching programmes, coaching engineering
  leaders since 2019
- **Languages:** English, Czech and Slovak
- **Based in** {{ site.author.location }}, working with clients across Europe
