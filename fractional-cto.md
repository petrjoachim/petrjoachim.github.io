## Sound familiar?

- "Engineering feels slow and I can't tell why."
- You need your first engineering managers and aren't sure who or how.
- The roadmap and what engineering delivers don't line up with product.
- You're a founder growing into the CEO role, and the tech side still runs
  through you.

## Advisory retainer {#advisory}

Monthly sparring for founders and CEOs. We meet regularly to work through
engineering, team and delivery questions, and you can reach me between
sessions when something comes up.

- Regular sessions, typically two a month
- Async access by message or email in between
- Reviews of plans, org charts, hiring decisions and technical strategy

**Example (anonymised):** a founder of a growing software company felt
engineering had stopped keeping up with sales. Together we mapped where work
was getting stuck, set up the first engineering manager role and agreed a
simple way to plan with product. Within a few months the founder was out of
day-to-day engineering decisions.

## Fractional CTO / VP of Engineering {#fractional}

Hands-on leadership one or two days a week, for companies between CTO hires
or scaling faster than their engineering leadership.

- Running engineering leadership meetings, planning and reviews
- Hiring and onboarding engineering managers
- Fixing delivery: release process, priorities and team setup
- Preparing the ground for, and helping hire, a full-time successor

**Example (anonymised):** a scaleup was growing its engineering team fast
while its release process still took months. I took over engineering
leadership part-time, reorganised teams around products and introduced
smaller, regular releases, cutting the release cycle to weeks.
